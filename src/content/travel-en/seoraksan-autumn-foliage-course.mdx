## At a glance

Seoraksan is known as one of the first places in Korea where autumn foliage begins. Color starts at the higher elevations and moves down to lower ground later in the season, so the best route depends heavily on when you visit.

## Different routes depending on timing

In early to mid October, higher-elevation routes like Daecheongbong Peak and Gongnyong Ridge are at their peak. By late October, the color moves down to lower areas around Biseondae and Sinheungsa Temple. If a full hike feels like too much, timing your visit for when the lower elevations peak is also a good option.

## Route characteristics

The Gwongeumseong cable car offers views of Seoraksan without hiking, suiting visitors who want to enjoy the foliage without physical strain. The Biseondae route follows a gentle walking path that families can manage without difficulty, while Daecheongbong Peak and Gongnyong Ridge are full hiking routes that require planning for a full day round trip.

## Before you go

- Check which elevation is at peak color for your visit dates
- Cable car operating hours and last-ticket cutoff
- Round-trip time if you choose a hiking route
- Warm, wind-resistant layers (temperature swings are large at higher elevations)
- Hiking boots and poles or other safety gear

## Trip planning notes

At Seoraksan, physical demand and time required vary a lot by route. Start by checking which elevation is at peak color during your visit window, then choose the route that matches &mdash; this order of planning cuts down on disappointment.
