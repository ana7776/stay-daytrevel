## At a glance

Naejangsan, a signature foliage spot in Jeongeup, North Jeolla Province, draws heavy search interest every autumn as people look for the exact peak timing. It's distinctive for having both a cable car and hiking trails, letting you choose a route to match your stamina and schedule.

## Checking the peak foliage timing

Naejangsan's foliage typically peaks from late October to early November in a given year, though that year's temperature and rainfall can push it earlier or later. It's safer to check real-time foliage updates from the local government or tourism information channels one to two weeks before your visit. Visiting on a weekday just before the peak lets you enjoy vivid color with a relatively relaxed route.

## Cable car route vs. hiking route

Naejangsan routes broadly split into two. The cable car takes you up near a viewpoint quickly, with less physical strain, suiting visitors who want to take in the foliage view in a short time. The hiking route, on the other hand, is especially popular along the section from Uhwajeong Pavilion through the foliage tunnel to Naejangsa Temple, and it can also connect to a walking route from the cable car drop-off point up to the summit.

## Before you go

- Check real-time foliage peak updates
- Cable car operating hours and last-ticket cutoff
- Parking congestion times (around 10 a.m. on weekends)
- Comfortable sneakers and a light jacket
- Expected hiking time (roughly 2–4 hours round trip)

## Trip planning notes

Since Naejangsan sees a search surge every foliage season, checking the peak timing ahead of time and scheduling a weekday morning visit is the most reliable way to avoid crowds. Rather than choosing only the cable car or only a hiking trail, combining both based on your stamina and remaining time is also a good option.
