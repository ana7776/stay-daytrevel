## At a glance

Kyoto, with its blend of temples and gardens, draws travelers from around the world every autumn for the foliage. Knowing the peak timing accurately makes a big difference to how satisfying the trip feels.

## Peak foliage timing

Kyoto's foliage typically peaks from mid to late November in a given year, though it can shift a week or two earlier or later depending on that year's temperatures. It's safer to check the latest foliage forecast before booking flights and accommodation.

## Timing differences by spot

Elevation and location can shift the timing at different temples and gardens by several days. If you're planning to visit several spots, rather than cramming them into one day, visiting in sequence over a few days can let you see a wider range of color.

## Before you go

- Check the latest foliage forecast before your trip
- Plan an early morning visit for popular temples
- Check in advance whether special night openings are available
- Wear comfortable shoes (expect a lot of walking within temple grounds)
- Book flights and accommodation with more buffer than your peak estimate
