## At a glance

Basing your Danyang stay in central Danyang-eup makes getting around easier for a first visit. Splitting your options by whether you're prioritizing Gosu Cave, Dodam Sambong, the Namhan River views, or a Sobaeksan itinerary makes the choice clearer.

## Areas to consider in Danyang

Central Danyang-eup is convenient for dining, amenities, and public transit access all together. If it's your first visit to Danyang or you're traveling without a car, it matters whether you can get dinner near where you're staying.

The area around Gosu Cave and the Namhan River makes it easy to stay close to the main sights. Routes combining Dodam Sambong, Gosu Cave, and the Mancheonha Skywalk are simple to fit into one day, though restaurants and amenities near your stay can vary by exact location.

Areas toward Sobaeksan suit travelers prioritizing hiking or nature over sightseeing. In that case, an early morning start, parking, and having breakfast or a simple meal ready can matter more than proximity to tourist spots.

## Getting around and access

Danyang's accommodation criteria differ depending on whether you're traveling by car or public transit. With a car, focus on parking and travel time between sights; by public transit, check Danyang Station, the intercity bus terminal, and taxi availability first.

Roads and parking near the sights can get busy during summer peak season and on weekends. When choosing where to stay, don't just look at the view &mdash; also factor in your route right after check-in, where you'll eat dinner, and travel time to your first stop the next day.

## Before you book

- Whether you have a car and whether your stay has parking
- How to get from Danyang Station or the bus terminal to your stay
- Travel time to Gosu Cave, Dodam Sambong, and the Mancheonha Skywalk
- Whether you have a Sobaeksan hiking plan
- Nearby restaurant and convenience store hours
- Check-in cutoff time and late-arrival policy
