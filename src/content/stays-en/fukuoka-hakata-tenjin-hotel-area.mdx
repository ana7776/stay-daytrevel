## At a glance

If you're looking for a Fukuoka hotel recommendation, it helps to first decide whether Hakata or Tenjin will be the center of your itinerary. Fukuoka's airport is close to downtown, so travel burden isn't huge either way, but your satisfaction can vary depending on whether you're prioritizing shopping, dining, or day trips.

## The difference between Hakata and Tenjin

Hakata is Fukuoka's main transit hub. If you need to juggle shinkansen, JR, subway, and bus connections, staying around Hakata Station keeps your itinerary simple. It also makes it easier to organize departures for day trips to places like Dazaifu, Kitakyushu, or Yufuin.

Tenjin is strong on shopping, restaurants, and cafes. With department stores, commercial facilities, underground malls, and the bus terminal clustered together, it suits travelers who spend a lot of time downtown from day into night. The area around the station can feel busier than Hakata, so it's worth checking whether your hotel is close to a main road.

Nakasu sits between Hakata and Tenjin, making for an appealing evening route. That said, some locations are close to the riverside nightlife district, so it's safer to check the map and reviews for late-night noise and the atmosphere around your hotel's entrance.

## Getting around and access

Getting from Fukuoka Airport into downtown is straightforward by subway. If you're arriving late or traveling with large luggage, check the walking distance from the station to your hotel, whether it connects through an underground mall, and whether there's an exit with an elevator.

If your trip is short, it's better to anchor around one area rather than switching hotels. Staying in Hakata reduces transit connections, while staying in Tenjin cuts down on the time it takes to get back after dinner and shopping.

## Before you book

- Airport travel time on your first and last day
- Which station is actually closest &mdash; Hakata, Tenjin, or Nakasu-Kawabata
- Whether you can walk there through an underground mall or along a main road
- How bright and busy the area is late at night
- Whether you have any day trips planned
- Room size, bed configuration, and the free cancellation window
