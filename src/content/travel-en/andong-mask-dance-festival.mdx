## At a glance

The Andong International Mask Dance Festival brings together traditional Korean mask dance performances and mask theater from around the world in a fall festival. Planning your route around the performance schedule lets you catch multiple shows without missing out.

## Checking the performance schedule

Various performances run in different time slots throughout the festival, so if there's a show you want to see, it's worth checking the schedule and planning your route in advance. Indoor and outdoor venues are sometimes separate, so factoring in travel time between them helps you enjoy the festival with less rush.

## Pairing with Hahoe Village

The festival grounds and Hahoe Village are separate locations that require travel time between them. If you want to see both in one day, splitting your time &mdash; Hahoe Village in the morning and the mask dance festival in the afternoon, for example &mdash; tends to work more smoothly.

## Before you go

- Check the schedule in advance for performances you're interested in
- Factor in travel time if pairing with Hahoe Village
- Look up public transit or shuttle bus information
- Bring warm, wind-resistant layers for outdoor performances
- Check whether hands-on programs need advance reservations
