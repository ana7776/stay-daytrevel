## At a glance

The Jinju Namgang Lantern Festival is a signature autumn festival where lanterns floated along the Namgang River light up the night. The mood shifts a lot between day and night, so how you time your visit shapes the experience.

## Lighting time and viewing highlights

Lanterns light up gradually right after sunset, and the display is at its most vivid once it's fully dark. The exact festival dates and lighting time shift slightly each year, so it's safer to check the official announcement before you go.

## Viewing route

Since the lanterns line the Namgang River around Jinjuseong Fortress, a common route pairs a visit to the fortress with a riverside lantern walk. There are several vantage points on bridges and along the riverside path where you can take in the lantern display, so moving between them for different angles is worth trying.

## Before you go

- The exact festival dates and lighting time for the year you're visiting
- Public transit or temporary parking on the outskirts
- A jacket for cooler night temperatures
- Extra time built into your route for crowds
- Whether tripods are allowed if you plan to take photos

## Trip planning notes

While the lit lanterns at night are the highlight of the Jinju Namgang Lantern Festival, visiting from the daytime to see Jinjuseong Fortress and the surrounding area, then catching the lanterns from dusk onward, makes for the fullest experience. On busy weekend evenings, build in extra time for getting around.
