## At a glance

Busan has indoor family spots spread across several areas, from the aquarium in Haeundae to the National Busan Science Museum in Gijang and the National Maritime Museum on Yeongdo. Since these can keep your itinerary going through rain, heat, or cold, they're worth keeping as a backup plan for a family trip.

## Busan's indoor spots, by type

Exhibit-focused venues include aquariums with large tanks of marine life, plus science museums and history museums with permanent and special exhibitions. These tend to have easier routes to walk and are generally stroller-friendly. For hands-on play, indoor kids cafes offer play equipment, which suits children who want to run around and be active. It's worth balancing exhibit time and play time based on your child's temperament and stamina.

## Planning a route by area

Busan's indoor family spots are easier to plan around if you think of them by area: Haeundae, Yeongdo–Nampo-dong, and Gijang. Haeundae has the aquarium and indoor kids cafes clustered together, pairing well with a beach visit. Yeongdo–Nampo-dong centers on the National Maritime Museum and connects well with a walk through the old downtown. Gijang, home to the National Busan Science Museum, fits a plan that heads a bit further out of the city. Moving between different areas in one day adds a lot of travel time, so sticking to about two spots within a single area is easier when you're traveling with kids.

## Before you go

- **Pick an area first**: choose whether your day is in Haeundae, Yeongdo–Nampo-dong, or Gijang
- **Balance exhibits and play**: adjust the mix based on your child's age and stamina
- **Getting around**: consider public transit too if weekend parking looks tight
- **Strollers and nursing rooms**: facilities vary by venue, so check each one's website beforehand
- **Nearby indoor options**: it helps to know nearby cafes or restaurants for a break
