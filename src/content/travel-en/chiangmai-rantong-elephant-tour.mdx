## At a glance

One popular way to meet elephants near Chiang Mai is the Ran-Tong Elephant Sanctuary tour in the Mae Taeng area. Rather than riding, the program centers on feeding the elephants, bathing with them, and giving them a mud spa &mdash; the no-riding approach is the main thing travelers check before booking. Since the day involves water and mud, it helps to plan your outfit and packing ahead of time.

## What a no-riding elephant experience looks like

Ran-Tong is known as a center devoted to caring for elephants that were overworked or are now elderly. Instead of riding or performance tricks, visitors prepare food and help bathe the elephants in the river, following the animals' own routines. After arrival, you change into local-style activity clothes and hear an introduction to the elephants and the sanctuary before the hands-on part begins.

Feeding starts with preparing fruit such as bananas or watermelon and handing it to the elephants, followed by a bathing session at the riverside. A mud spa follows, and it's worth keeping in mind that your clothes and shoes can get quite wet and dirty during this part.

## Half-day vs. full-day, pickup, and what to pack

Programs are usually split into half-day and full-day options. The half-day option runs in a morning or afternoon slot and focuses on feeding and bathing, which suits travelers who want to split the day with other plans. The full-day option adds time walking with the elephants and observing their natural behavior, plus a local meal, using the whole day.

Ran-Tong is in Mae Taeng rather than central Chiang Mai, so travel time is required. Most bookings include round-trip pickup from hotels in the city, but pickup coverage can vary, so it's safer to check before booking. Since the day includes water play and a mud spa, packing a spare set of clothes, sandals you don't mind getting wet, basic toiletries, sunscreen, and insect repellent will help.

## Before you go

- **Choose a program**: half-day vs. full-day changes the scope of activities and whether a meal is included
- **Pickup coverage**: pickup availability and timing can depend on your hotel's location
- **Animal welfare policy**: riding and bullhook use vary by operator, so confirm before booking
- **Spare clothes and shoes**: bathing and the mud spa will get your clothes and shoes wet
- **Weather and season**: river conditions or the schedule may change during the rainy season, so check before booking
